A list of all substantial courses which I attended during my graduate studies in MA Public Policy & Management at Universität Potsdam from 2010 to 2013.

#### Winter term 2010/2011

Field:[economic sociology](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_sociology), [varieties of capitalism](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varieties_of_Capitalism) | taught by [Prof. Sorge](http://www.wzb.eu/gwd/into/people/sorge.de.htm)

As part of the course, I produced a [handout on convergence among multi-national enterprises](/uploads/THAPA-Varieties-of-Capitalism.pdf) (German).

Field: sociology of public administration | taught by [Prof. Edeling](http://www.uni-potsdam.de/ls-apelt/emeriti.html)

As part of this course, I produced a [presentation on privatization of water services](/uploads/THAPA-Privatisierung-der-Wasserversorgung.pdf) (German).

Field: [public management](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_management) | taught by [Prof. Proeller](http://www.uni-potsdam.de/u/ls_puma/index.php?article_id=21)

As part of the lecture, I produced a [case study on change management](/uploads/THAPA-Fallstudie-Change-Management.pdf) and a [presentation on public accounting systems](/uploads/THAPA-Oeffentliches-Rechnungswesen.pdf) (both in German).

Field: [development studies](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_studies) | taught by [Prof. Fuhr](http://www.uni-potsdam.de/fuhr/team/fuhr.html)

#### Summer term 2011

Field: [public management](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_management) | taught by [Prof. Proeller](http://www.uni-potsdam.de/u/ls_puma/index.php?article_id=21)

Field: [public management](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_management), [case study research](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_study) | taught by [Prof. Proeller](http://www.uni-potsdam.de/u/ls_puma/index.php?article_id=21)

As part of the project, I produced a [presentation on multi-case study research](/uploads/THAPA-Ragin-Turning-the-Tables.pdf) (in German).

Field: [institutional ethnography](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institutional_Ethnography), [sociology of law](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology_of_law) | taught by [Dr. Scheffer](http://www.law-in-action.de/team/scheffer/)

#### Winter term 2011/2012

Field: [public management](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_management)

A mock consulting job for []init[ AG](http://www.init.de/en), where my group did a study on mobile citizen services in German municipalities.

As part of the seminar, I produced a [presentation on associations as interest groups](/uploads/THAPA-Interessenvertretung-durch-Verbaende.pdf) (in German).

Field:[e-government](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-government) | taught by [Dr. Proske](https://www.linkedin.com/pub/niels-proske/5a/ab8/b1)

As part of the seminar, I produced a [presentation on apps and portals in m-government](/uploads/THAPA-Portal-oder-Apps-M-Government-Praesentation.pdf) (in German).
