I started my print design activities in 2000, when I was hired to design the magazine "Nepal Information". Since then, I have worked on several yearbooks at my highschool, held a job as a print designer at university during my undergraduate studies and realized more paid and unpaid freelance design jobs than I can remember.

Here is a growing collection of those projects that I am still aware of.
I don't have the files and rights to all of them but I try to show examples as far as possible.
