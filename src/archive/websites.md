Websites I built or helped build between the early 2000s and the early 2010s, mostly as a freelancer or for friends and family. Many have since been redesigned, moved or taken offline, so the links may lead somewhere quite different today.

### QigongRaum
[qigongraum.de](http://www.qigongraum.de) · WordPress

A lean website for Martha Maria von Hugo’s Qigong lessons, without any fancy features. What brings it to life is the background image shot and montaged by Jan von Hugo.

### Chalet Sunnehang
[sunnehang.de](http://www.sunnehang.de) · WordPress

A small, no-frills website for a friend’s chalet in Switzerland, with minimal plugins and a lightly customised theme.

### Zoon Politikon – Werkstatt für Politik & Wirtschaft
[zoon-politikon.de](http://www.zoon-politikon.de) · WordPress

Deliberately “undesigned” until a proper logo and corporate design existed: the imageless Twenty Eleven theme with PT Sans as the only extravagance.

### Nepalese Honorary Consulate General, Cologne
[konsulatnepal.de](http://www.konsulatnepal.de) · PHP, later WordPress

One of my first real web design projects. I later migrated it to WordPress with a clean theme and an improvised [random Flickr slideshow](/blog/2010-05-10-a-random-flickr-slideshow-for-wordpress/) to make it a little more lively.

### MikrofinanzWiki
[mikrofinanzwiki.de](http://www.mikrofinanzwiki.de) · Drupal

Several part-time months of building wiki features and a lightweight community in Drupal. The community concept was later dropped in favour of more controlled content, but the basic structure remained.

### Lahure Kitab | Edition Kathmandu
[editionkathmandu.de](http://www.editionkathmandu.de) · Joomla, later WordPress

Website and web shop for our publishing house. In 2005, a distinctive design and a shop in Joomla meant plenty of headaches; it later moved to WordPress with a cleaner look.

### Albertus-Magnus-Gymnasium Bensberg
[amg-bensberg.de](https://www.amg-bensberg.de) · self-made CMS

My school’s website started with a small homepage team that my friend Peter and I set up, running on a home-made CMS. Its content structure outlived us for years.

### NGO Forum Nepal
[ngo-forum.de](http://www.ngo-forum.de) · custom PHP

Built during my most enthusiastic PHP days on a completely custom platform with a simple internal CMS.
