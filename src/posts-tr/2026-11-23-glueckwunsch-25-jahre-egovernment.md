---
title: Congratulations on 25 years of eGovernment
---

The trade magazine *eGovernment* celebrates its 25th anniversary with issue 12/2026. Companions from government, business and academia sent their congratulations, among them Andreas Steffen, deputy chair of the NEGZ board, and myself on behalf of the NEGZ:

> “Hard to believe: with eGovernment there is a forum for debating digital government that is even older than the NEGZ! Thank you for your tireless reporting on this connoisseur’s topic and for the good collaboration in helping the analyses and ideas from the NEGZ reach the professional community. Here’s to the next 25 years together!”

The double page with all congratulations is available as a PDF (in German): [eGovernment 12/2026, pp. 8–9](/uploads/press/egovernment-2026-12-25-jahre.pdf).
