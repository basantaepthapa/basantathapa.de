A condensed version of my [Master thesis](/en/studies/) on the role of local chambers of commerce and industry for democratic consolidation in the Philippine Visayas and North Mindanao regions has been published in *[ASIEN - The German Journal on Contemporary Asia](http://asien.asienforschung.de/asien-138-januar-2016/).*

*ASIEN* is the official publication of the German Association for Asian Studies. This young researchers' special issue of *ASIEN* collects the best papers of the [Junior Researcher’s Conference of the German Association for Asian Studies](http://asiennachwuchs.de/tagungen/2015) at Burg Rothenfels in Germany in January 2015.
