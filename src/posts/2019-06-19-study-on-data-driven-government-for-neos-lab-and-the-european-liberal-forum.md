In May 2019, [Neos Lab](https://lab.neos.eu/), party think tank of the Austrian liberal party [Neos](https://www.neos.eu/), invited me to author a study on data-driven government for them. The idea was to assess data-driven government, meaning the use of algorithmic decisionmaking, Artificial Intelligence, and the like in the public sector, from the perspective of liberal political philosophy.

The study is funded by the [European Liberal Forum](https://www.liberalforum.eu/), think tank of the liberal European party family.

As I considered the research question as well as the challenge of breaking the technical and philosophical aspects down for policymakers (who are the target group of these publications) highly interesting, I gladly accepted the invitation.
