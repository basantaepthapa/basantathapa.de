In early 2014, I the [ENSA program](https://ensa.engagement-global.de/) for school exchanges between Germany and developing countries contracted me to evaluate their impact in 2013.

To this end, I read and coded almost 200 student reports from 25 exchange projects to assess which skills and knowledge areas improved as a result of the exchanges.

I reported my evaluation in a dense 20-page report to the ENSA office.
