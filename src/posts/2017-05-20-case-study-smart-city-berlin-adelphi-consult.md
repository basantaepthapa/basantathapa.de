In Summer 2017, the Berlin-based sustainability consultancy [adelphi consult](https://www.adelphi.de/en) contracted me to produce a case study on [Smart City Berlin](https://www.berlin.de/sen/wirtschaft/en/economics-and-technology/centres-of-technology-future-locations-smart-city/smart-city/artikel.442220.en.php), i.e. Berlin government's concepts and efforts to turn Berlin into a [smart city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_city). The case study is part of a [larger report](https://difu.de/11127) on smart cities in Germany for Deutsche Telekom AG which adelphi co-produces with [Deutsches Institut für Urbanistik](https://difu.de/).
The case study will be based on official documents and interviews with relevant actors in city administration, business, academia, and civil society.
