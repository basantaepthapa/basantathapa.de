*[eGovernment Review](http://www.egovernment-review.org/),*a non-peer-reviewed open access journal on electronic government run by [FH Kärnten](https://www.fh-kaernten.at/) in Austria, published a short two-page article by [Niels Proske](https://www.linkedin.com/in/niels-proske-0b1ab85a) and me. It is based on a [term paper](/uploads/THAPA-Portal-oder-Apps.pdf) I wrote for a seminar on e-government which was taught by Niels Proske.

The article problematizes whether classic all-purpose e-government portals can be succesfully ported to mobile devices such as smartphones and tablets which are dominated by apps with limited purposes and functionalities.
