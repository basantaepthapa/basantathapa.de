In December 2020, I hosted a panel discussion at the [Nordl@nderDigital](https://www.nordlaender-digital.de/fachforen/) web conference on the topic of artificial intelligence in government.

The Nordl@nderDigital conference adresses the digitalisation of government in the northern German federal states.

For 1,5 hours, I pestered my three panelists with questions on AI and automation in public administration:

- [Daniela Kolbe](https://daniela-kolbe.de/), Member of the Bundestag and chair of the [Bundestag Study Commission on Artificial Intelligence](https://www.bundestag.de/ausschuesse/weitere_gremien/enquete_ki)
- Regine Schlicht, head of [Mittelstand 4.0-Kompetenzzentrum Kiel](https://www.digitales-kompetenzzentrum-kiel.de/) and board member of [Digitale Wirtschaft Schleswig-Holstein Clustermanagement](https://www.diwish.de/)
- Dr. Jesper Zedlitz, officer at the [Schleswig-Holstein Ministry of Energy, Agriculture, the Environment, Nature and Digitalization](https://www.schleswig-holstein.de/EN/StateGovernment/V/v_node.html)
