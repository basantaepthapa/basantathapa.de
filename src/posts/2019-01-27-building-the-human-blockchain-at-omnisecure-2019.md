In January 2019, I facilitated a workshop on the blockchain at the [Omnisecure 2019](https://www.omnisecure.berlin/en/) conference in Berlin. Omnisecure is an annual conference on "smart identity solutions" such as eIDs, digital payment, blockchain, connected living, etc.

I was invited to be the game master of the Human Blockchain, a gamified workshop that lets participants experience how distributed ledger technologies work without having to understand any complex IT concepts.

While there are still a few kinks to work out and improvements to make to the workshop concept, feedback was very positive, and I look forward to testing refined versions on future occasions.
