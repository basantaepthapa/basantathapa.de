My [bachelor thesis](http://www.basantathapa.de/studies/papers#graduate "papers"), which received its final touches at a kitchen table in Cebu City, deals with the rather philosophical question: "How revolutionary is microfinance really?". Although not directly dealing with questions of sustainability, the thesis still made a solid 5th place at [Oikos International](http://www.oikos-international.org/)'s [Bachelor Thesis Award.](http://wittenherdecke.oikos-international.org/projekte/award0.html)

[![oikos Bachelor Thesis Award](/uploads/2014/10/oikos-certificate-1024x721.jpg)](/uploads/2014/10/oikos-certificate.png)

As part of the award, my thesis was published on the [Oikos Bachelor Thesis Award website](http://wittenherdecke.oikos-international.org/projekte/award0/gewinnerinnen-2010/5-platz.html), along with a [three-page summary](http://www.faktorn.de/wp-content/uploads/2010/12/oikosBAaward_2010_5th.pdf) which may be more digestible to the interested reader than [the full 43 pages](http://basantathapa.de/studies/term-papers/#undergraduate).

Further, Oikos PhD fellow [Tim Lehmann](http://www.oikos-international.org/academic/fellowship/current-fellows/tim-lehmann.html) arranged an Skype interview with me.
