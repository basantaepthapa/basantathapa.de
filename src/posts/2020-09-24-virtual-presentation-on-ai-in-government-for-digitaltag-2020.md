On 19 June 2020, the first nationwide [Digitaltag](https://digitaltag.eu/) ("digital day") was held in Germany. Organisations from all sectors sessions on various aspects of digitalisation.

My employer, the [Fraunhofer Institute for Open Communication Systems](https://www.fokus.fraunhofer.de/en), participated with five online talks on different aspects of digital government. See the institute's program [here](https://www.fokus.fraunhofer.de/de/dps/events/digitaltag_2020).

I contributed a twenty-minute talk on opportunities and issues of artificial intelligence in government. followed by a short Q&A. Watch my talk here (in German):
