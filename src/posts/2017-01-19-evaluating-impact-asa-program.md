In early 2017, me and my companion Jacob Wunderwald successfully tendered for the impact assessment of the [ASA program](http://en.asa-programm.de/english/home/). We were contracted to conduct the assessments one and five years after individual participation in the ASA program.

The assessment is carried out with online surveys which are to be improved, sent out and finally analysed.
