In October 2015, I tought [a block seminar on qualitative research methods](https://www.hs-merseburg.de/fileadmin/fb_smk/Downloads/VVZ_WiSe_2015_16.pdf#page=50) as an adjunct in Merseburg. The course was part of the Bachelor program in Social Work, which is one of the flagship degrees of the [Merseburg University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hs-merseburg.de/en/university/).

Since I held the seminar in English, only a tiny group of students found their way into the course. As a result, it was a very intense experience and rather fortunate for me, as I had to devise my prepared course contents several time to adjust them to the students' previous training.

Also, Merseburg is a surprisingly beautiful town which I would not have visited otherwise.
