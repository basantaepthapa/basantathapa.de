In July 2018, I was invited to talk about issues of Artificial Intelligence in public administration at the [10th Bayrisches Anwenderforum](https://www.bayerisches-anwenderforum.de/Nachlese-2018-910293.html) in Schloss Nymphenburg in Munich.

The congress convenes mostly civil servants from the state of Bavaria who are interested in matters of digitalisation. My panel dealt with questions of Artificial Intelligence and included a talk about guiding principles of AI for public use and a use case of IBM Watson for data analytics by a Swiss police department.
