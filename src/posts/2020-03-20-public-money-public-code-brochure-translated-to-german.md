In March 2020, the German translation of the [Public Money Public Code brochure by the Free Software Foundation Europe](https://fsfe.org/news/2019/news-20190124-01.en.html) was published. The publication explores the potential of Free Software for the public sector, taking on misconceptions.

In 2019, I had [contributed a short article](/en/blog/2019-02-02-how-to-procure-free-software-contribution-to-the-public-money-public-code-campaign/) to the English original and later on [presented the brochure at various occasions](/en/blog/2019-03-08-talking-public-money-public-code-at-netzpolitischer-abend/).

Over the course of 2019, I organised the translation and design of the German edition. I even ended up adding the finishing touches with the [Free desktop publishing solution Scribus](https://www.scribus.net/). As a result, the German edition of the brochure is co-published by my employer [ÖFIT](https://www.oeffentliche-it.de/).

You can read the German translation, which is licensed under CC BY, here:
