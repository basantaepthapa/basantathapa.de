[Facebook groups](http://www.facebook.com/groups) are a great way to stay up-to-date about topics and circles of people that you like. However, without the right settings, your precious groups might end up cluttering your email inbox with notifications.

Before you take desperate measures and leave your groups altogether, try this little guide. It shows you how to stay informed about a specific group on Facebook while keeping it from spamming your email inbox.

(There is a plethora of similar guides on the web but apparently Facebook made some changes, so every single one I checked out was obsolete.)

# Step-by-step

Make sure you're logged in on Facebook and you're on your groups page. This is where we start.

## Step 1

Find the "Notification" box at the top right corner of your group page. Click it, then choose "Settings".

[![](/uploads/CropperCapture1.png "CropperCapture[1]")](/uploads/CropperCapture1.png)

## Step 2

A decision box pops up. Do not temper with the "Notify me about" switch since this might inhibit your notifications about the group in Facebook.

Instead, we want to "Change your email frequency settings". Click it.

[![](/uploads/CropperCapture2.png "CropperCapture[2]")](/uploads/CropperCapture2.png)

## Step 3

Welcome to your "Notification Settings" page. You might have also gotten here via your "Account Settings", but the route above is more intuitive.

Look for the "Email Frequency" checkbox in the top right corner. If this is checked (which might be a good idea if you want to minimize Facebooks impact on your inbox), uncheck it now. You can turn it back on later but is has to be unchecked for our further steps.

[![](/uploads/CropperCapture3.png "CropperCapture[3]")](/uploads/CropperCapture3.png)

## Step 4

Further down on that website, find the settings for "Groups". Click "Edit".

[![](/uploads/CropperCapture4.png "CropperCapture[4]")](/uploads/CropperCapture4.png)

## Step 5

There is a bunch of checkboxes that manipulate *global* settings for all of your groups. You might want to adjust this to your tastes.

However, we are here to get rid of **all** the emails from a specific group, so let's move down to "Change email settings for individual groups". (If you were too clever to follow step 3, this option won't be there.)

[![](/uploads/CropperCapture5.png "CropperCapture[5]")](/uploads/CropperCapture5.png)

## Step 6

We're finally here. A box pops up which lists all of your groups. By unchecking the box next to the group, you will stop all email traffic from this group to your email account.

[![](/uploads/CropperCapture6.png "CropperCapture[6]")](/uploads/CropperCapture6.png)

## Step 7

Reward yourself. Enjoy your new lean email inbox.

Oh, and before you leave the "Notification Settings" page, don't forget to turn the "Email Frequency" checkbox back on (step 3) if you want the summary.

PS: This guide is valid for Facebook as it is in January 2012. If you can't find some of the options I point at, Facebook may have just changed the menu structure again. Or maybe you should read step 3 more closely.
