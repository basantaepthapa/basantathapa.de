On 26 May 2020, I attended an ["Introductory workshop for the development of AI indicators in work and society"](https://www.denkfabrik-bmas.de/en/projects/ai-observatory/introductory-workshop-for-the-development-of-ai-indicators-in-work-and-society) by the German AI Observatory at the Policy Lab Digital, Work & Society at the Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs. At the workshop, experts from various organisations brainstormed how metrics for the progress of AI could look like.

The AI Observatory has now published a blog post with their take on the results of our discussions:

![](https://www.denkfabrik-bmas.de/fileadmin/_processed_/9/9/csm_KI-Trends-in-Arbeit-und-Gesellschaft-gestalten-Teaserbild_79aafee9fb.png)

### Shaping AI trends in work and society - an evidence based approach is crucial

AI has an enormous impact on work and society. Political action is required, which needs to be supported by a solid evidence base on AI in work and society. The German AI Observatory is building this evidence base.
