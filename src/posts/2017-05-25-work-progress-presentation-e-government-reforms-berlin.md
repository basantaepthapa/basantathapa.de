On 24 May 2017, I gave a presentation at the [FoJuS Annual Conference 2017](https://www.dvpw.de/gliederung/sektionen/spv/fojus/fojus-jahrestagung-2017.html) at the University of Potsdam presenting the current progress of a book chapter on e-government reforms in the state of Berlin.
At that point, the project was rich in data but still undertheorized, so the critical questions and constructive comments from the audience provided valuable and inspiring input to the paper.
