In December 2018, I held a workshop on the topic "Whose knowledge counts? Legitimacy of Big Data-driven politics" at the [WZB Mercator Forum 2018.](https://www.wzb-mercator-forum.de/forum/forum-2018/)

The WZB Mercator Forum is a two-day science-policy transfer format at the [Berlin Social Science Center (WZB),](https://www.wzb.eu/en) funded by [Stiftung Mercator](https://www.stiftung-mercator.de/en/). The 2018 edition of the forum dealt with "Big Data 4 Policy – Experimental policy advice and -making with Big Data".

My workshop was based on my book chapter ["Vier wissenspolitische Herausforderungen einer datengetriebenen Verwaltung"](http://publica.fraunhofer.de/dokumente/N-503864.html) and tried to foster discussion and interaction among the participants by having them come up with examples for the different knowledge political dynamics of Big Data in policymaking.
