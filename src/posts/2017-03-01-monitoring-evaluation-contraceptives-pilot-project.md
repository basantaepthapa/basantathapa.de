[![](/uploads/2017/05/Logo_biko.jpg)](/uploads/2017/05/Logo_biko.jpg)I have been contraced for the monitoring & evaluation of a pilot project concerning contraceptives together with my colleague [Jacob Wunderwald](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jacob-wunderwald-053955136/?ppe=1).

The project "[biko](https://www.bmfsfj.de/bmfsfj/aktuelles/alle-meldungen/kosten-fuer-verhuetungsmittel--beduerftige-frauen-entlasten/111656)" ("Zugang zu verschreibungspflichtigen Verhütungsmitteln. Kostenübernahme, Informationen und Beratung für Frauen mit Anspruch auf Sozialleistungen") pilots the reimbursement for prescription contraceptives for low-income women. It is implemented by [pro familia](https://www.profamilia.de/pro-familia.html), the largest NGO for sexual and reproductive health and rights in Germany and funded by the German [Federal Ministry for Family Affairs, Senior Citizens, Women and Youth](https://www.bmfsfj.de/bmfsfj/meta/en/en).

We are tasked with the quantitative monitoring, including developing the monitoring process and tools, user support for the monitoring tools, and analysing the collected data. Further, we will develop estimates for the nationwide demand based on the data from the seven pilot locations.

Our client is [Camino - Werkstatt für Fortbildung, Praxisbegleitung und Forschung im sozialen Bereich](http://www.camino-werkstatt.de/), a Berlin-based social work consultancy and the project runs from 2017 to 2019.
