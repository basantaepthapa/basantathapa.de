On the occasion of the [Internet Governance Forum 2019](https://www.igf2019.berlin) being held in Berlin in November 2019, I orchestrated a special issue on Internet Governance in the Bremen-based student-run social science journal [KEY ISSUES](http://key-issues.eu/), guest edited by ÖFIT.

I've been active as a reviewer for KEY ISSUES for a few years and was happy to take the special issue to them.

Please submit and/or share, if this call is something for you or people you know!
