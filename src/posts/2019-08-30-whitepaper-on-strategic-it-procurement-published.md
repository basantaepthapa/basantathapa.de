In August 2019, ÖFIT published a whitepaper I had written on the strategic procurement of IT, especially non-standard software solutions.

The study retraces the traditional lines of argumentation around goals of public procurement and decision models in procurement management. From this, I suggest a wider and more holistic strategic perspective for IT procurement decisions that take into account a number of dimensions such as dependency from vendors, user acceptance, and sustainability concerns.
