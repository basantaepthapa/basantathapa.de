In summer 2018, I taped a long interview on digitalisation, artificial intelligence and Big Data for the online format Erfurter Stubenexpress.

Erfurter Stubenexpress is produced by the local political party ["Mehrwertstadt Erfurt"](http://www.mehrwertstadt-erfurt.de/), in which some friends of mine are involved. When they had heard about my edited volume [“(Un)berechenbar? Algorithmen und Automatisierung in Staat und Gesellschaft”](/en/blog/2018-06-07-edited-volume-unberechenbar-algorithmen-und-automatisierung-staat-und-gesellschaft-published/), they wanted to include this perspective on digitalisation in their program.

The Stubenexpress is hosted in a miniature living room on the back of a pickup truck, in our case in the ruins of the guard house of the Thuringian data center during socialist times.
