In April 2020, the first episodes of the ÖFIT podcast, the government think tank I work at, were published. These included an episode where I talked about Artificial Intelligence in government in general and our [scenario study "Exekutive KI 2030"](/blog/2018-09-12-scenario-study-on-ai-in-the-public-sector-published/) in particular.

You can listen to the episode (in German) here:

![](/uploads/2020/05/oefit-podcast-logo.jpg)

In the first episode, our director Peter Parycek talks about ÖFIT and its approach to thinktanking in general. In another episode, my colleague Resa Mohabbat Kar talks about digital-ready legislation based on our [impulse paper on machine-consumable laws](/blog/2019-09-28-impulse-paper-on-machine-consumable-legislation-published/).

All of the episodes are available [at the ÖFIT website](https://www.oeffentliche-it.de/oefit-podcast) and via your favorite podcatcher.
