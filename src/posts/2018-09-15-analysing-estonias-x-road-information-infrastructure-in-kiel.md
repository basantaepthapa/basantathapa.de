On 13 September 2018, I joined the academic conference ["Wissenschaftliche  Konferenz Staatsmodernisierung und Verwaltungstransformation"](https://negz.org/2018/08/06/wissenschaftliche-konferenz-staatsmodernisierung-und-verwaltungstransformation/) on e-government at the state parliament in Kiel.

The conference was held by three German research institutions working on digital government: The [German Research Institute for Public Administration (FÖV)](http://www.foev-speyer.de/en/index.php?p_id=17) in Speyer, the [National E-Government Competence Center (NEGZ)](https://negz.org/ueber-uns/wer-wir-sind/), and the [Competence Center for Public IT (ÖFIT)](https://www.oeffentliche-it.de/), my employer.

My talk explored X-Road, the Estonian public information infrastructure, and its fit for the German public sector. The presentation was based on the interviews I led during my research stay in Tallinn in winter 2017.

Despite some technical difficulties (The projector failed exactly when I wanted to show a crucial schematic of the X-Road system.), the presentation sparked a lot of interest and I received valuable recommendations on which directions to continue this project in.
